04 / BUILD THE PROPOSAL

A strong proposal covers four dimensions.

01

Market Analysis

Research the brand and its market, then explain the need and feasibility.

02

Solution Design

Propose a sustainable solution linked to the SDGs or measurable indicators, including resources and constraints.

03

Technology

Explain how technology supports the solution. If AI is used, include prompts or screenshots as evidence.

04

Expected Outcomes

Describe benefits, impact, potential challenges, and response strategies.

FAQ

Before you enter, check the essentials.

Who is eligible?＋

Current National Taipei University students, including degree and non-degree students. Teams may have 1–4 members, and each student may register only once.

Are the training sessions mandatory?＋

Yes. Each team must send at least one representative to the blockchain workshop, and every participant must attend at least one brand briefing. Missing these requirements will generally be treated as an incomplete competition process.

Can a proposal combine different brands?＋

No. You may select one challenge or combine both challenge directions from your assigned brand, but proposals may not cross brands.

Is attendance at the awards ceremony required?＋

Yes. The ceremony is an official part of the competition and will take place on Dec 29, 2026, from 13:00 to 14:00 at the USR HUB on the Sanxia Campus.
